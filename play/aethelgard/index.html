    Arena law

    Each duelist fights as their saved hero: vocation, level, gear and talents. Echoes are hardier than flesh, so both begin with double health. No draughts, no feathers. Turns alternate; a hero who lets the sand-glass run out guards by reflex, and one who idles twice yields. Victory brings glory alone: a mark on your record.

    Duels link your browser directly to your friend's. A public PeerJS signal tower only introduces you; your friend code is the only name it sees.

    Local & Duel Network — Top 25 / Top 5 boards store your record and anyone you have dueled. Snapshots merge on a successful friend-code connect. There is no central global server on GitHub Pages.
